# Group Buy Refund Customer Tutorial — Video Design

## Purpose

Create a concise tutorial showing a Group Buy member how to request and monitor a refund entirely from the customer portal.

## Format

- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Duration: approximately 55 seconds
- Narration: calm voiceover with matching on-screen captions
- Audience: Group Buy members
- Scope: customer point of view only
- Data: clearly fictional customer, order, wallet, and transaction details

## Recommended Treatment

Use a guided product walkthrough that recreates the relevant customer portal screens. Cursor movement, focused zooms, highlights, and short callouts should direct attention to each action. Avoid admin screens and internal implementation details.

## Storyboard

1. Title: “How to request a Group Buy refund”
2. Open the customer portal and navigate to My Orders.
3. Select the relevant confirmed Group Buy order.
4. Locate and select “I want a refund.”
5. Explain the available refund methods:
   - Account credit
   - Crypto when enabled for that Group Buy
6. For crypto, confirm the displayed asset and network, then enter the destination wallet.
7. Review the refund amount and submit the request.
8. Explain the customer-visible statuses:
   - Requested
   - Being verified
   - Verification failed
   - Completed
9. Explain that crypto completion may take time while blockchain confirmations arrive.
10. Close with: “You can return to the order at any time to check the status.”

## Visual Direction

- Match the Peps Anonymous customer portal’s visual identity.
- Keep the interface legible at video resolution.
- Use one persistent progress motif to connect the steps.
- Use smooth camera pushes and interface-focused transitions rather than static slides.
- Keep captions inside title-safe margins and synchronized with narration.

## Accuracy Requirements

- Show the refund action only on an eligible confirmed Group Buy order.
- Do not imply that crypto refunds are always available.
- Do not imply that submission means completion.
- Distinguish outgoing transaction verification from the customer’s original payment.
- Do not expose real customer, order, wallet, or transaction information.

## Deliverables

- Runnable video artifact
- Exported MP4
- Voiceover and synchronized captions included

## Acceptance Criteria

- A member can understand where to request a refund and what happens afterward.
- No admin interface or internal operational process appears.
- The full video remains understandable with audio muted.
- Text is readable at 1920×1080.
- Playback and exported MP4 complete without blank frames or missing assets.
