Record-centric hierarchy

The group buy is the primary record. Its fields, lifecycle, actions, activity, and related data remain predictable on every screen.

2

Enterprise navigation

Global tools, object navigation, record tabs, and contextual actions each have one consistent location.

3

Dense, readable surfaces

Compact 8px cards, restrained shadows, aligned field grids, and standard list views make high-volume work efficient.

4

Operational activity

Every payment, parcel, message, COA, status change, and dispatch action becomes part of a visible audit timeline.

Professional component rules

Card geometry8px radius, 1px neutral border, almost no decorative shadow.
TypographyInter 700 for record titles, 600 for headings/actions, 400–500 for fields.
Action placementPrimary actions stay top-right; row actions stay at the end of each record.
Information densityUse field grids, tables, and related lists instead of oversized dashboard cards.
