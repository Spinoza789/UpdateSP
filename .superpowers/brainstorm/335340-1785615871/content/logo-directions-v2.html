Salt&Peps — three professional logo directions

Designer conventions applied: single idea per mark, flat colour, optical spacing, tested reversed & at app-icon size. Palette: ink navy #16243D + muted gold #A16207 (trust + premium, WCAG-checked). Click the direction you want developed.

A — Apothecary crystal

Salt-crystal rhombus holding a single gold drop. Quiet, premium, "lab-grade" without cliché molecules. Type: DM Sans, tracked capitals. Most premium/trust direction.

B — The peptide bond

Three linked residues ascending — literally a peptide chain, and a "pooling together" metaphor. Type: Satoshi bold lowercase. Most modern/product-led direction.

C — Editorial wordmark

No icon — high-contrast Bodoni serif with an italic gold ampersand and a hairline rule. Boutique, fashion-house confidence; the ampersand becomes the brand shorthand. Type: Bodoni Moda.
