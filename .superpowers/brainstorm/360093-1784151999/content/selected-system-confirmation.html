Responsive behaviour

  • Desktop sidebar becomes a drawer below 1024px.
  • Desktop tables become action cards below 768px.
  • Filters open as a mobile bottom sheet.
  • Every touch control is at least 44px.

Surface and colour rules

White cards, slate borders, restrained shadow, 12–14px radii and Peps navy for selected or primary actions. Status colour remains semantic, never decorative.
