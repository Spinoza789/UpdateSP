Three distinct ways to run the member directory

All three sit inside the unchanged GB Organiser V2 shell and preserve the same metrics, filters, sorting, row actions, and member details. Click any direction you especially want me to emphasize; you are not choosing a winner yet.

01 · Fastest follow-up

Command Ledger

A dense, confident directory with a pinned attention rail for unpaid, stalled, or unresolved members.

02 · Richest context

Member CRM

A master–detail workspace where the selected member’s status, orders, notes, and history stay visible beside the directory.

03 · Best overview

Signal Board

A cohort-first dashboard that turns payment, fulfilment, geography, and participation into scan-friendly signals above the directory.

Shared contract: identical member records, responsive behaviour, accessible controls, live-feeling search and filters, sortable columns, and an actionable detail view.
