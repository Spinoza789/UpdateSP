Working read for SALT&PEPS

The strongest path is probably a clean, bold wordmark with one custom intervention in the ampersand or final S. The board should help us choose the temperature of that intervention: utilitarian, gently human, premium, or clinical. No tagline and no forced icon are needed at this stage.
