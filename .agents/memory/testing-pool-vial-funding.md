---
name: Testing pool vial funding semantics
description: Why extra-vial pricing belongs to the pool goal rather than contributor charges
---

Pool leaders choose a planned vial count, a per-extra-vial price, and the maximum vial count for their standalone testing pool. The first vial is included in the test prices; extra vials add to the shared target **once for the whole pool**, regardless of the number of tests or contributors. A contributor's opt-in amount is independent of the per-vial rate.

**Why:** A pool collects variable or fixed contributions toward one funding goal. Treating each additional vial as a personal add-on, or multiplying the surcharge per selected test, would overcharge contributors and make the goal disagree with the leader's breakdown.

**How to apply:** Keep the saved target, leader/admin previews, and public breakdown on the same pool-level calculation. Do not mix this standalone-pool pricing with separate Janoshik milestone or group-buy testing rules.
