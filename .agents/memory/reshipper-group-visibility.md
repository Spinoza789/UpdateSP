---
name: Reshipper group visibility
description: Personal hiding scope and restoration requirements for reshipper group buys.
---

Reshippers can hide group buys from their own panel and restore them later; the preference must remain saved across sign-ins and devices.

**Why:** The user requested hiding old group buys, confirmed that hiding is personal to the reshipper's panel, and explicitly required the choice to save.

**How to apply:** Never treat hiding as removal of assignments, orders, or access. Do not share this preference with the customer group-buy archive feature. Always keep an accessible way to show and restore hidden groups, including when every group is hidden.
