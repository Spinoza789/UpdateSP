---
name: Supplier testing policy
description: User decisions about JBG testing group scope and eligibility
---
JBG supplier testing is grouped per product and physical batch, not one group per supplier shipment. Members can join as soon as a wholesale, shared-wholesale or group-buy order is placed, whether paid or unpaid. Members need contextual join buttons and a Supplier Testing account tab.

**Why:** The user requested all three order flows and explicitly selected product-and-batch grouping and participation as soon as ordered.

**How to apply:** Keep supplier testing eligibility separate from product payment and testing-contribution payment; do not substitute shipment batches for product batches or combine suppliers' results.
