---
name: Supplier testing policy
description: User decisions about JBG testing group scope and eligibility
---
JBG supplier testing is grouped per product and physical batch, not one group per supplier shipment. Members can join as soon as a wholesale, shared-wholesale or group-buy order is placed, whether paid or unpaid. Members need contextual join buttons and a Supplier Testing account tab.

**Why:** The user requested all three order flows and explicitly selected product-and-batch grouping and participation as soon as ordered.

**How to apply:** Keep supplier testing eligibility separate from product payment and testing-contribution payment; do not substitute shipment batches for product batches or combine suppliers' results.

Unknown physical batches collect free interest until staff explicitly assigns the purchased item to a supplier/product/batch group. Do not infer the purchased batch from current stock. Keep assigned memberships and contribution history when an order later changes.

**Why:** A catalogue's current batch is not proof of which physical batch fulfilled a historical order; automatic matching could present unrelated results or transfer financial history.

**How to apply:** Require an explicit batch assignment before offering the group's contribution/results flow. Multiple qualifying orders must share one account-owned membership for that exact batch.

Supplier batches use Admin-owned pools only. Community pool leadership must never grant supplier batch access or member-participant management privileges.

**Why:** Owning a community pool does not establish that someone purchased, was assigned to, or is authorised to manage a particular supplier batch.

**How to apply:** Member-facing batch access requires explicit assignment even if an account matches an old pool leader; supplier batch administration stays on authenticated Admin routes.
