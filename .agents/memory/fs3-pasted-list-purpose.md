---
name: FS3 pasted supplier lists
description: The user's intended purpose and price source for external product-and-quantity lists.
---

The user wants to paste an external list of items and quantities and apply their saved FS3 supplier costs, so the resulting priced list can be sent to their supplier.

**Why:** They explicitly chose saved FS3 supplier costs rather than customer selling prices or prices entered for each list.

**How to apply:** Treat this as preparation of a supplier-facing list, not import of new catalogue prices, a customer quote, or permission to place an order.

Pasted-list downloads should use the full wholesale TXT format, including shipping, recipient address and phone.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the same format as a wholesale download and chose to include editable delivery details.

**How to apply:** Keep copied and downloaded text consistent; do not invent an order reference or delivery details for an external pasted list.
