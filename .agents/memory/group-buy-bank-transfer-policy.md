---
name: Group-buy bank transfer policy
description: Organiser bank-transfer fields and manual receipt-confirmation boundary.
---

Group-buy organisers may offer UK-to-UK and international bank transfer alongside Revolut and PayPal. UK details include sort code, account number and bank name. International details include account number/IBAN, SWIFT/BIC, optional clearing/routing code, and the bank's full name and address.

**Why:** The user requested these two bank-transfer options and approved a member payment option with manual organiser confirmation.

**How to apply:** A member saying they sent a transfer only requests review; it must never mark the payment paid or imply blockchain verification. Show the order's actual currency, not a relabelled USD amount.
