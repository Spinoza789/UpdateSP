---
name: Wholesale fulfilment wording
description: Meaning of the second wholesale/shared-order milestone.
---

For wholesale and shared wholesale orders, the second fulfilment milestone is “Order received by Vendor”, not “Processed” or “Processing”. The sequence is Submitted, Order received by Vendor, Shipped, Delivered.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this wording. It describes vendor receipt, rather than implying internal packing or processing.

**How to apply:** Treat this as customer-facing wording, not a change to persisted statuses or processing behaviour. Do not rename milestones for unrelated shop or group-buy orders.
