Do not send automatic unpaid-order/payment reminders through the Telegram bot, for any group-buy status or order type.

**Why:** The owner reported reminders for group buys that were no longer open and explicitly requested removal of unpaid Telegram notifications, not merely a closed-group filter.

**How to apply:** Keep automatic unpaid reminders disabled even if historical template or scheduler settings enable them. Preserve other bot notifications and member-requested order/payment information.
