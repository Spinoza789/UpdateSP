---
name: Single-vial pricing currency
description: Seller-currency checkout policy and the boundary between order prices and crypto settlement.
---

Keep single-vial prices in the seller's chosen currency throughout shopping and checkout. Show the converted USDT/USDC payment separately, locked when the order is placed. Do not combine differently denominated items into one numeric total.

**Why:** The user approved this approach after a sterling-priced listing was shown as the same numeric dollar/USDT amount in the basket. Currency conversion and relabelling are not interchangeable.

**How to apply:** Preserve pricing and settlement denominations independently. A later seller currency edit must not reinterpret existing orders. Do not guess the pricing currency of historical orders that never recorded it, or change already locked payment amounts to make the display look consistent.
