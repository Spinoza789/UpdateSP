---
name: Member group-buy completion
description: Personal Completed lists for group buys and orders are distinct from fulfilment and order receipt.
---
Members mark group buys completed for their own accounts only, with restore available. The member list has All, Open, Closed and Completed filters.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose “Each member—for their own account only,” rather than Admin/Organiser completion for everyone.

**How to apply:** Keep this separate from the shared group-buy status, supplier batches, progress tracker, payment status and individual order receipt. Completion must never change another member’s view or fulfilment state.

The order list also separates active and personally completed orders, with restore available and an All order-type filter within either section. Treat this as list organisation, not the shipped-only “Mark as received” workflow.

**Why:** The user requested an order-completed button to move orders into a completed section and reduce the crowded list, immediately after choosing personal group-buy completion.

**How to apply:** Preserve actual fulfilment/payment statuses and account-saved persistence. Never treat moving an order into this personal list as proof of delivery or payment.
