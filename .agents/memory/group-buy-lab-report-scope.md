---
name: Group-buy lab report scope
description: User-approved scope for choosing a single certificate for a group-buy product.
---

Admins and organisers must be able to choose one certificate for a product within one group buy, without changing that product's reports elsewhere.

**Why:** The user wanted HGH 36iu to show one report rather than all HGH reports, and explicitly chose “This group buy only”.

**How to apply:** Keep certificate choices scoped to the group buy. Do not turn an individual group's certificate selection into a global product rule.
