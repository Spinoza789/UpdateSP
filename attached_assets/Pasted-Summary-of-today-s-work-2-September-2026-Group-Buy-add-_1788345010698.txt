Single-vial shipping mode
Added an optional, Group Buy-specific calculation mode within Shipping Split:

Organisers can enable single-vial adjustment when needed.
Multiple products can be selected as single-vial products.
Normal shipping is calculated first.
Selected vial products receive one-tenth of their normal full-kit shipping share.
Mixed orders keep normal products at their regular shipping portion.
The remaining shipping is redistributed across the other eligible orders.
Added handling for vial-only orders, mixed orders, and multiple selected vial products.
If the allocation cannot balance, the shortfall remains visible and Apply is disabled.

The calculation wording was corrected to clearly distinguish kits from vials:

2.87 per full-kit ÷ 10 = 0.287 per vial
0.287 per vial × 3 vials = 0.86

The calculation now explicitly shows the quantity multiplier and avoids describing the initial amount as a “vial portion.”
